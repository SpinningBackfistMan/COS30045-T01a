Appliance Energy Consumption in Australia

Household appliances account for a large share of home electricity use in Australia. This site will showcase data visualisations exploring how much energy common appliances use and how Energy Rating labels help consumers choose efficient products.

Energy Rating Label

Stars from 1 to 10 show how efficient an appliance is compared with others of the same size.

kWh per year

The label's number estimates yearly electricity use under standard test conditions.

Save money

A more efficient appliance can cut running costs every year it is used.

Televisions

Televisions sold in Australia must carry an Energy Rating label. Screen size and technology (LCD, LED, OLED) strongly affect power consumption.
